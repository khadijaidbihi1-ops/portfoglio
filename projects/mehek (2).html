Case study 01

MEHEK

A luxury fragrance e-commerce experience inspired by heritage, craftsmanship and contemporary design.

RoleUI Design & Frontend Development
TechnologiesHTML, CSS, JavaScript
StatusOngoing
MEHEK

Overview

The challenge

MEHEK was created as a refined e-commerce concept for a fragrance brand combining Moroccan, Pakistani, Italian and London influences.

The goal was to build a responsive experience with clear navigation, distinctive collections and user-friendly product interactions.

Solution

Design approach

The visual direction uses restrained colours, editorial typography, generous spacing and product-led imagery.

The development includes responsive layouts, product filtering, collection navigation and cart interactions using vanilla JavaScript.
